## 1. Budowanie Baz Danych Firm

### Brief facts

Client: `Stereomatic Agency`

Scope:
- long-term cooperation building B2B company/contact databases for prospecting campaigns;
- client handles outreach; freelancer delivers database only;
- company list according to criteria supplied after NDA;
- contacts for specified job-function groups;
- each record: first name, last name, position, corporate email, domain, source;
- manual deduplication and gap-closing using company sites, LinkedIn, KRS, rejestr.io;
- tools mentioned: Apollo, Hunter, FullEnrich, Clay; client may provide access plus manual research.

Selection path:
1. offer + portfolio + answers;
2. short questionnaire;
3. paid trial on a small batch;
4. selection of 2–3 freelancers;
5. NDA and cooperation.

Questions asked in application:
- historical number of records / industries / sample / process / clients / experience;
- tools and monthly limits;
- email verification method, bounce rate and missing-address handling;
- daily throughput and pricing model;
- confidentiality experience.

Conditions:
- confidential target criteria and ready database;
- no resale / competitor use;
- personal-data work subject to GDPR/RODO.

Visible sent offers: about `13`.

### Fit

`CAPABILITY FIT = HIGH`

Strong match with:
- web research;
- structured data;
- source traceability;
- deduplication;
- completeness checks;
- manual gap-closing;
- clean spreadsheet/database delivery.

### Credential risk

`CREDENTIAL FIT = MEDIUM`

The client asks for historical throughput, bounce rate, tools and prior experience. Those must not be fabricated.

Honest position if no prior commercial database-building history:
- do not claim historical record counts or bounce rate;
- present process and capability;
- state tool access truthfully;
- use paid trial to establish real throughput/quality;
- optional small sample can demonstrate method without pretending it was client work.

### Sensitive-data / legal note

Named B2B contact details are personal data. The client explicitly references GDPR/RODO and confidentiality. Any application/work should preserve source/provenance and avoid inventing contact data. The client should remain responsible for lawful campaign use; the freelancer should work only to the defined scope and public/provided sources.

### First-revenue verdict

`APPLY CANDIDATE = YES, WITH HONEST POSITIONING`

This is the strongest of the three for first paid evidence because it has:
- concrete deliverable;
- paid qualification task;
- moderate visible competition;
- recurring work potential;
- no explicit degree/certification gate;
- client-provided tool access may reduce setup cost.
