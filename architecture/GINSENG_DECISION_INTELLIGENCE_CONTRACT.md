## Definition

```text
GINSENG = DECISION INTELLIGENCE LAYER
```

Ginseng is a versioned system of decision memory and change-impact analysis.

It preserves:

- decisions and their owners;
- premises and sources;
- considered and rejected alternatives;
- dependencies and functions;
- expected and observed consequences;
- uncertainty;
- Decision Lineage.

It is not a graph visualization product, dashboard, CMDB, execution engine or owner of canonical truth.

## Frozen semantics

- `FACT / DECISION / HYPOTHESIS` remain separate;
- important decisions preserve Decision Lineage;
- relation authority and source remain visible;
- relevant impact can be explained through `ELEMENT → FUNCTION / CAPABILITY → EFFECT`;
- AI cannot promote its own hypothesis or relation to confirmed truth;
- Creative OS owns canon;
- Ginseng does not execute changes.
